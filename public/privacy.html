Privacy Policy

MRHS FBLA Prep · Last updated 4 October 2026

MRHS FBLA Prep is a study tool built by and for the Marvin Ridge High School chapter of FBLA. It is not a commercial product. This policy explains what it stores and what it does not.

What the app stores

  • Your name, your school email address, and the FBLA event you compete in. These are entered by a chapter officer when your account is created.
  • Your answers to practice questions, including which ones you got wrong.
  • Which topics you have practised and how you are doing in each, so the app can show your progress and bring missed questions back for review.

What the app does not do

  • It does not show advertising.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone outside the chapter.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not collect your location, your contacts, your photos, or anything from your device beyond what you type into it.
  • It does not ask for payment and has no in-app purchases.

Who can see your results

Your answers and progress are visible to you. Chapter officers who run the app can see chapter-wide practice activity so they know which events need more material. Your account is protected so that signing in as you is the only way to read your own results.

Where the data is kept

Account and practice data is stored with Supabase, a hosted database provider, and the app itself is served by Vercel. Both are United States providers. Question explanations are generated using Anthropic's Claude API; only the question text is sent for that, never anything about you.

Students under 13

The app is for high school students. Accounts are created by chapter officers for FBLA members, and no account is created for anyone under 13.

Deleting your data

Ask a chapter officer or the chapter adviser to delete your account. Your account, answers and progress are removed. There is no copy kept afterwards.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the Marvin Ridge High School FBLA chapter adviser, or to the chapter officers through the contact details on the chapter page.

MRHS FBLA Prep is a student project of the Marvin Ridge High School FBLA chapter. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by FBLA-PBL, Inc., and is not an official FBLA product.
